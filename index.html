About us

Simulacrum Interactive is an indie game studio based in Lethbridge, Alberta focused on making non-traditional games across a variety of styles, genres, and platforms.

MULTIPLAYER ARENA BATTLER – IN DEVELOPMENT

Inspired by the fast-paced movement and escalation of an epic anime battle, Brobots is a kinetic multiplayer brawler game centered around the Brobots: a color coded cast of friends with their own unique abilities. Customize your playstyle with cards that grant abilities, buffs, gadgets, traps and more as you fight for the favor of the Cybergod in a retrofuturistic Y2K inspired world.

✦ Kinetic Movement Defy gravity and move like an anime character from wall running to flips and mid-air battles.
↗ Escalating Action Power scales from the beginning of the match to the end with cards that grant buffs, abilities, traps, equipment and more.
◇ Retrofuturistic Y2K Aesthetic A fresh take on the ‘look of the future’ as imagined at the turn of the millennium.
◉ Multiplayer Mayhem Casual ‘drop in and play’ fun with elements of mechanical depth and strategic deck building.

VR ADVENTURE PLATFORMER - 2018

Traverse the fractured scapes to test your skills, uncover the stories of the ancients, or explore the mystical world around you in search of secrets. Etherian is a zen VR adventure / platformer where the player is free to explore at their own pace. Focus on the puzzles and obstacle courses, hunt for hidden secrets, or uncover the story behind the fractured scapes of Etheria.

↗ VR Physicality Climb, swim, jump, zip-line – interactions are physical, using VR to the fullest.
◇ Fractured Scapes of Etheria Travel through the zen, liminal biomes of an ancient land from fire and ice to greenery and sand.
◉ Exploration Follow the path or veer from it to see what you can find.
△ Mystery What happened to Etheria and its inhabitants? What lessons can be learned from the past?

HALO FANGAME – 2022 / ONGOING

Built in the original DOOM engine and constructed with a mix of captured, recreated, and hand-crafted elements from the Halo titles, Halodoom is a mosaic of the imagery and feel of early Halo games. Rather than a 'demake' of any one Halo game, Halodoom is a standalone game that pays homage to the entire franchise while bringing new content to the universe. Offline multiplayer (bots) only with full multiplayer and campaign planned for the future.

↗ Time Capsule of Halo Maps, weapons, textures and skyboxes that harken back to and sample from all eras of the Halo franchise.
◇ The Halo Feel Each part of the game is made to feel and play like Halo, but through the limitations of a much more primitive game engine, something new emerges!
◉ Customization Customize your Spartan with 8 different armor coatings combined with a slider system for primary color, customize your firefight experience with hidden skulls.
△ Slayer Try out 72 different gamemode combinations including Slayer, SWAT, Oddball and many others against custom bots made to simulate a competitive online multiplayer environment.
PLATFORMS PC
RELEASE DATE 2022 / Ongoing
GENRE FPS / Fangame
