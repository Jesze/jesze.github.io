MULTIPLAYER ARENA BATTLER · IN DEVELOPMENT

Brobots is a fast-paced third-person multiplayer arena battler where highly mobile cyborgs clash across a strange virtual world built for explosive, movement-driven combat. Wall-run, grapple, launch through the air, and tear across arenas on a hoverboard while adapting your build with cards that shape your movement, defense, gadgets, and playstyle.

✦ EXPLOSIVE ARENA COMBAT Fast third-person battles built around mobility and knockback

VR ADVENTURE-PLATFORMER

Etherian is an adventure-platforming game with puzzle solving elements where you will have to jump, swing, swim, slide, and climb your way through the beautiful floating world of Etheria. Traverse the fractured scapes to test your skills, uncover the stories of the ancients, or explore the mystical world around you in search of secrets.

↗ PHYSICAL TRAVERSAL Climb · Swim · Throw · Zip-line · Hop
◇ FOUR SCAPES Orchard · Desert · Winter · Lava
◉ ANCIENT RECORDS Discover Etheria through glyph tablets
△ EXPLORATION Hidden paths, hand patterns, secrets
PLATFORM PC VR
MODE Single Player
WORLDS Four Scapes

HALO FANGAME · DOOM ENGINE

Halodoom is a fangame that pays tribute to the Halo universe and seeks to push the Doom Engine to its limits! While utilizing the source port Zandronum, Halodoom offers a totally unique and standalone modern Halo experience within the classic engine that originally spawned the first person shooter genre!

↗ HALO-LIKE COMBAT Halo 2/3-inspired pacing and gunplay
◇ PUSHING DOOM 3D models · Dynamic lights · 3D floors
◉ EXPANSIVE MAPS Large spaces beyond classic Doom
△ ALTERNATE EVOLUTION Halo pursued through Doom
PLATFORM PC
ENGINE Zandronum
